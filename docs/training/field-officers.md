# Course for field officers (half a day)

By the end, each officer has downloaded a project, captured features with no connection, synced them, and answered ground-truthing tasks.

**Each officer needs:** an Android phone with the app installed, charged, and an account. **The trainer needs:** a project on the training server with a planning area around the outdoor area, a buildings layer and a points layer (with one required field and one pick-list), and some existing features to check.

Guide: [The field app](../user/field-app.md).

## Indoors (1 h 30)

### 1. Sign in and download (20 min)
Everyone signs in (server address on the board), downloads the project with both layers, and opens the map.
*Check:* each phone shows the layers and the dashed planning-area line.

### 2. Working with no connection (15 min)
Turn on **airplane mode**. Close the app fully and open it again. Sign in.
*Check:* everyone gets back in and sees the map. Explain: the password check now happens on the phone.

Explain the plain background: outside map services can't be stored on a phone; the Assembly's own drone image can.

### 3. Capturing (30 min)
Demonstrate each way, then everyone does each once, indoors by drawing:
- a point drawn on the map;
- an area drawn by tapping corners;
- the form: leave the required field empty and try to save; pick from the list; take a photo.

*Check:* each officer has 2 captures in their list (top right of the map).

### 4. What the numbers mean (15 min)
- **±3 m** means the true position is probably within 3 metres. Under trees or beside tall buildings it gets worse.
- A GPS point is the average of 5 readings: **stand still** until it finishes.
- Readings worse than ±50 m are ignored. If nothing is good enough, move into the open.
- A phone is good for buildings and facilities. It is not a survey instrument.

### 5. Personal details (10 min)
Don't write names, phone numbers or ID numbers in notes. Don't photograph people. If a form has no field for something, tell the planner rather than putting it in notes.

## Outdoors (1 h 30)
Stay in airplane mode.

### 6. GPS points (30 min)
Capture 5 points from GPS at things the trainer names (a gate, a standpipe, a signboard), each with a photo.
*Check:* each shows an accuracy and "5 readings".

### 7. Walking (30 min)
Walk a line along a path, and the edge of one building or compound as an area. Pause halfway and resume.
*Check:* the shape on the map follows where you walked.

### 8. Checking what's recorded (30 min)
Open the clipboard list. For three features the office has sent: go to each, and answer correct as recorded, needs correcting (fix the form), or not found.

## Back indoors (45 min)

### 9. Sync (20 min)
Airplane mode off. **Sync.** Read the summary together.
*Check:* "nothing to send" on a second sync. The trainer shows the captures on the projector, with who recorded each and the photos.

### 10. When things go wrong (15 min)
- Start a sync and switch to airplane mode partway: read the message. Switch back and sync again: it carries on.
- The trainer changes a feature an officer has edited but not synced. The officer syncs and sees "conflict: with the office". Explain: nothing is lost; a planner decides.
- A capture refused by the server: open it, read why, fix it, sync.

### 11. Looking after the data (10 min)
- **Sync at the end of every day** when you have a connection.
- Until you sync, the captures exist only on the phone. Don't uninstall the app or clear its data.
- Sync before installing an update.
- A lost phone: tell the district administrator the same day.
