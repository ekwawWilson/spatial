# Trainer's checklist

## A week before
- [ ] A **training server**, separate from the live one, installed and reachable from the training room ([deployment](../ops/deployment.md)). Never train on live data.
- [ ] A training district created, with one account per trainee: planners as *planner*, one or two as *district administrator*, field officers as *field officer*, and one *viewer* account to demonstrate with.
- [ ] The practice files copied to a shared folder or USB sticks: everything in `fixtures/generated/`.
- [ ] For the field course: the app installed on every phone, phones charged, and the outdoor area chosen (open sky, safe to walk, 5 to 10 things to capture).

## The day before
- [ ] Sign in as a trainee from a trainee's computer, on the training room's network.
- [ ] Do exercise 1 of each course yourself, start to finish.
- [ ] On one phone: sign in, download a project, turn on airplane mode, open the map.
- [ ] Projector and printed manuals.

## On the day
- [ ] Write the server address and the Wi-Fi details on the board.
- [ ] Pair people if computers are short, and swap who types after each exercise.
- [ ] Let trainees make mistakes: every change can be undone or restored from history. Show that early.

## Afterwards
- [ ] Note what people found hard, and tell the developer.
- [ ] Remove or keep the training district, as agreed. Deactivate trainee accounts that shouldn't stay.
