# Committees

*Everyone can see the meetings. Planners and district administrators plan meetings and keep the minutes; district administrators set the members.*

The District Spatial Planning Committee decides; its Technical Sub-Committee checks, inspects and reports (L.I. 2384 r.44). Choose **Committees** in the left menu.

## Members
Under **Members**, list each committee's members and the office each holds. The quorum depends on particular offices being present (r.7):

| Committee | Quorum | Offices that must be present |
| --- | --- | --- |
| District Spatial Planning Committee | 9 members (for approving a plan) | the DCE (or the Coordinating Director in the DCE's absence), Physical Planning, Development Planning, Works, Disaster Prevention, Lands Commission, EPA, Roads |
| Technical Sub-Committee | half its members | Physical Planning, Development Planning, Works, Lands Commission, EPA, Roads |

## Planning a meeting
Choose the committee and the date, then **Plan the meeting**. The agenda is built from what is waiting for that committee:
- **Technical Sub-Committee:** permit applications received or with it, and re-zoning, change of use and subdivision applications under consideration.
- **Spatial Planning Committee:** applications the Sub-Committee has reported on, local plans whose draft has been on display, and the land applications.

Applications that arrive later are added with **Add what has come in since**.

## Notice (Form 5)
Members are given written notice **at least three days before** the meeting (r.6). **Download the notice**: it carries the agenda and the members. Then press **Notice served today**; the page shows a notice served late, or not served by the last day, in red.

## At the meeting
- Tick each member **present**. The page says whether the **quorum** is formed and, if not, which office is missing.
- For each item, choose the **outcome** (recommended for grant or refusal, granted, refused, approved, deferred, noted) and write the **minute**. Each application's title links to its page, where the decision itself is recorded.
- **Download the minutes**: attendance, quorum and every item's outcome and minute, ready to sign.
