# Enforcement

*Planners and district administrators; field officers can open cases.*

The platform keeps a **case** for each contravention, issues the notices the Regulations provide for, records how each was served, and works out the dates that follow (Act 925 s.160–167; L.I. 2384 r.30, r.55–58). It can also compare two drone images to find development nobody applied for. Open a project and choose **Enforcement**.

## Finding new development from the air
Under **Find new development from the air**, choose two orthophotos of the same place taken at different times and **Compare**. The platform marks where the ground changed and sorts each place into one of three kinds:

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New: no permit and no building on the map** | Look at these first: development that neither a permit nor the map explains |
| **At a building already on the map** | An extension, a new roof, or a change beside a known building |
| **Where a permit has been granted** | Explained by a granted or issued permit |

The places go into a layer called **Changes found between images**, coloured by kind, on the project's map.

A comparison is a place to look, not a finding. Light, shadow, the season, a parked lorry or a cleared plot also change a picture. Changes smaller than about 16 m² are ignored. Use images of similar quality, and send an officer before opening a case.

To check each change against permits and buildings, the project needs its **properties** layer set under Relationships, and its permit applications in the platform.

## Opening a case
Choose **Open a case**: the contravention, how it came to light, what was found, and the owner or occupier if known. To tie the case to something on the map (a building, a parcel, or a change found between images), give its **feature number**: click it on the map to read the number.

| Contravention | Law |
|---|---|
| Development without a permit | Act 925 s.117 |
| Breach of a condition of a permit | Act 925 s.160 |
| Does not conform to the approved zoning scheme | Act 925 s.84 |
| Encroaches on public space or a right of way | Act 925 s.121 |
| Untidy land; dangerous building; other | Act 925 s.160 |

The case gets its number: district, EN, year and a running number, for example `SMA/EN/2026/0007`.

## Notices
On the case, choose the notice, type what has come to the Assembly's attention and what must be done (one point per line), and give the date to comply by. Then **Download the notice**: it is worded after the Schedule's form, with the property described at the end, ready for the Chief Executive's signature.

| Notice | Form | When |
|---|---|---|
| Stop Notice or Order for Discontinuance | 48A | Work must stop at once. It has no date to comply by. |
| Notice for Rectification of Contravention | 49 | The owner is to put it right by a date. |
| Enforcement Notice | 47 | The owner did not rectify in time. |
| Notice of Revocation of Permit | 48B | A condition of a permit is breached. Link the case to the permit first. |
| Notice to Enter Land for Enforcement Purposes | 50 | Before an authorised officer enters. |
| Planning Contravention, Breach of Condition, Untidy Land, Dangerous Buildings notices | — | The other notices the Regulations name (r.55(2)). |

### Serving a notice
Record **how** it was served, by one of the ways the Regulations recognise (r.56): posted on the land or building, on the Public Data Room's notice board, on the Assembly's notice board, sent by post or left at the last known address, published in a national newspaper, emailed, or sent by another form of written communication.

### The days that follow
The case shows what is due, and overdue steps are in red.

| After | The Regulations say | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| The date to rectify passes without rectification | Serve an enforcement notice within **14 days** | r.57(2) |
| An enforcement notice is served | After **14 days** the Assembly may rectify and recover the cost | r.57(4) |
| A notice to enter land is served | Entry not before **3 days** | r.58 |
| A revocation notice is served | The permit holder has **21 days** to show cause | Form 48B |

## Closing a case
- **Rectified by the owner:** the notices are marked as complied with.
- **Rectified by the Assembly:** only once 14 days have passed since an enforcement notice was served. Record the cost and how much has been recovered from the person responsible (r.57(4)).
- **No contravention found.**
