# Managing district members

*For district administrators.*

Open **Members** to see everyone with access to the current district.

## Roles
| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| District administrator | everything in the district, including members and the audit log |
| Physical planner | work on plans (from Phase 3) |
| Field officer | capture data in the field (from Phase 9) |
| Viewer | look, but not change |

## Adding someone
Enter their email, optionally their name, choose a role and select **Add member**.
- If they don't have an account yet, one is created and they're emailed a link to set a password.
- If they already have an account (for example in another district), they simply gain access to this district.

## Changing a role or removing someone
Change the role in the list, or choose **Remove**. Removing someone keeps their history in the audit log; **Restore** gives them access again.

A district must always keep at least one district administrator, so you can't remove or demote the last one.
