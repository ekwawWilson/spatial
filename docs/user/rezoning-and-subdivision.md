# Re-zoning, change of use and subdivision

*Everyone can see the applications. Planners and district administrators receive them and record the steps. The decision is the district administrator's to record.*

Some applications change the land itself and not a building on it. The Regulations set a procedure and time limits for each, and this page keeps them: open a project and choose **Re-zoning and subdivision**.

| Application | Law | Form | Public comments | Decision due |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Re-zoning | L.I. 2384 r.33; Act 925 s.93 | 33A and 33B | 28 days from display | 60 days from the applicant's reply to objections |

## Receiving an application
1. **Receive an application**, and choose what it is for.
2. Give the applicant and the **parcel id** (several, separated by commas; a consolidation needs two or more). The parcels must be on the map, in the layer set as the project's parcels (Relationships, Layers).
3. For a **re-zoning**, give the zone asked for, and why: to comply with the structure plan, or a special circumstance (r.33(2) and (3)). The present zone is read from the map.
4. For a **change of use**, give the present use and the use asked for. For a **subdivision**, into how many plots.

The application gets its number (`SMA/RZ/2026/0001`; `CU`, `SD` and `CN` for the others) and the days start to run from the day it was received.

## Steps and deadlines
Each application shows its steps in order, with the rule each comes from, the date it is due, and the date it was done. Press **Done today** when a step is taken; add a note, such as the newspaper's name. A step done after its date is marked late, and a step not done by its date is shown in red, here and in the list of applications.

**Re-zoning (r.33)**
- Displayed in the Public Data Room and on the Assembly's notice board: 7 days.
- First publication in a local daily newspaper: 7 days. Second publication: within 28 days of the first.
- The applicant directed to give notice to the owners of abutting land: 7 days.
- Notice posted at three places near the land.
- Objections referred to the applicant: within 14 days of the end of the comment period.
- Decision: within 60 days of the applicant's reply. Where nobody objected, the platform counts the 60 days from the end of the comment period.
- If the decision is **deferred**, the applicant is told why and it is decided within 30 days (r.33(16) and (17)).
- Once approved: recorded, published in the Gazette and lodged in the Public Data Room within 7 days.

**Subdivision and consolidation (r.54)**
- Receipt acknowledged: 7 days. Advertised in a newspaper circulating in the District: 21 days.
- A copy to the Lands Commission for comment, and the advertisement displayed in the Public Data Room.
- The applicant told of what was received, with 14 days to respond.
- Decision: 60 days from receipt.
- Once approved: published in a newspaper and lodged in the Public Data Room; notice served on the Lands Commission within 30 days.

Some steps appear only when they apply: the referral of objections once an objection is recorded, and the steps after approval once the application is approved.

## Owners of abutting land
For a re-zoning, the page lists **the parcels that touch the land**, read from the map, with each owner where the parcel layer records one. These are the owners the applicant must give notice to on Form 35. Download **Notice to the owners of abutting land** and hand it to the applicant. Where an owner is not on record, the Regulations allow two newspaper publications or a notice posted near the land instead (r.33(9)).

Owners' names are shown only to those who may see personal data.

## Comments and objections
Record each comment, objection or representation as it arrives (Form 59), and the Lands Commission's comment on a subdivision. Then record the applicant's reply to each. An **approval cannot be recorded until the comment period has ended**; a refusal can.

## A change of use: the tests of r.34
A change of use may be allowed only where the development:
- is within the permissible uses of the zoning scheme;
- does not significantly alter the original intention of the plan or zone;
- has minimal impact on existing services and infrastructure;
- does not significantly increase traffic, noise or odour, or the risk of fire or explosion, or undermine the image of the area;
- intrudes on no one's privacy, or the intrusion is of net benefit to the community.

Mark each **Met** or **Not met**, with the reason. Every test must be assessed before a decision, and all must be met before an approval is accepted.

## The decision, and the map
Record **Approve** with any conditions, or **Refuse** with the reasons. Download the decision to send to the applicant.

When a **re-zoning is approved**, choose **Write (the zone) to the map**: each parcel's zone changes to the new one, as one recorded change per parcel, so the permit checks and the zoning register use it from then on. If the project draws its zones as a separate zoning layer, redraw the zone there too.

An approved subdivision or consolidation is drawn with the map's own split and merge tools; download the **Notice to the Lands Commission** to serve on its local office.

## What the public sees
Once an application is displayed or advertised, and the project is published in the [Public Data Room](public-data-room.md), it is listed there with what is asked for, the last day for comments, and the decision. Applicants' contact details and commenters' names are not shown.

## Papers
- **Public notice of the application:** for the notice board, the newspaper and the three places near the land.
- **Notice to the owners of abutting land** (re-zoning).
- **Decision.**
- **Notice to the Lands Commission** (an approved subdivision or consolidation).

They carry what the Regulations require each to say. When the Assembly supplies its own forms and letterhead, they replace these layouts.
