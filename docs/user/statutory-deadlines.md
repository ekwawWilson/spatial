# Statutory deadlines

*Everyone in the district: the department to plan its week, management to see how the Assembly is doing.*

The Regulations give the Assembly dates for much of its planning work. **Statutory deadlines**, in the menu, counts them all from the platform's own records, for your district, as of today.

## What it counts
| Duty | The limit | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Permit applications | Acknowledged in 7 days, inspected in 14, reported in 21, decided (and certified where granted) in 30 | L.I. 2384 r.44(6)–(10) |
| Requests for information to the Public Data Room | Answered in 5 days | r.64(7) |
| Enforcement cases | An enforcement notice within 14 days of the date to rectify passing | r.57(2) |
| Re-zoning, change of use and subdivision | Each step of the application: display, publication, referral of objections, decision, gazetting, notice to the Lands Commission | r.33, r.34, r.54 |
| Zoning schemes | Prepared within 90 days of the structure plan; a copy to LUSPA within 28 days; updated on schedule | r.29 |

For each you see how many are **open**, how many are **past their date** (in red), and, for permits and requests, **how often the limit was met this year**, with the average number of days from receiving an application to deciding it.

## Using it
- **Past a deadline:** each late application, case or scheme is listed with its step, its date and how many days late it is. Click one to open it.
- **Due in the next seven days:** the permit steps coming up, so nothing becomes late unnoticed.
- **Download as a page to print:** the same figures as a PDF, for management meetings and as evidence for the District Assembly Performance Assessment Tool (DPAT).

The figures are counted each time the page opens. They are only as complete as the records: an application kept on paper and never entered is not counted.

No personal information appears on this page, so every member of the district can see it.
