# Your work, search and setting up

*Everyone in the district: what to do next, and how to find a record quickly.*

## The Home page: your work
**Home** lists everything in the district that has a next step, the most pressing first. It covers every module:

| Record | The next step shown |
|---|---|
| Permit applications | The first statutory step not done: acknowledge, inspect, report, decide (r.44) |
| Reviews and appeals | The decision, due 21 days after it was lodged (r.62(3), r.63(8)) |
| Re-zoning, change of use, subdivision | The next step of the application (r.33, r.34, r.54) |
| Enforcement cases | Serve a notice, serve an enforcement notice, check compliance, close (r.57) |
| Requests for information | Answer within 5 days (r.64(7)) |
| Committee meetings in the next 14 days | Serve the notice (Form 5) three days ahead (r.6(2)) |
| Zoning schemes | A duty past its date (r.29) |

Each row shows how late or how soon the step is (**5 days late**, **Due in 2 days**, **No date**), the record's number (click it to open the record), the step, and who is handling it.

The buttons above the list choose what you see:
- **Assigned to me**: the records handed to you. The list opens here when you have any.
- **Late**: everything past its statutory date.
- **Due this week**: due in the next seven days.
- **Everything**: the whole list. Use **All kinds** to show one kind of record only.

## Handing a record to an officer
Permit applications, land applications and enforcement cases can each be handed to an officer: a planner, a field officer or the district administrator. Choose the name in the row's **officer** box on the Home page, or under **Officer** on the record's own page. Choose **Not assigned** to take it back. The change is recorded in the audit log.

Who can hand records out: those who process permits (planners and administrators) for permit and land applications; those who act on enforcement for cases.

## Searching
The **search box** in the top bar finds any project, permit application, land application, enforcement case, request for information, rateable property or street name by its number or name. Press **/** anywhere to jump to it, type at least two letters, use the arrow keys and **Enter** to open the result.

Applicants' and owners' names are searched only for the roles that may see personal information (planners and administrators).

## Setting up a new district
District administrators see **Setting up the district** on the Home page until every step is done, each linked to where it is done:
1. Choose the district's coordinate system.
2. Add the planning staff.
3. Create the first plan project.
4. Add a background map.
5. Name the committees' members.
6. Set the permit fees.
7. Set the property rate bands.
8. Open the Public Data Room.

## In the field app
Each project on the phone's first screen now shows three counts you can tap: features **to check** on the ground, permit **inspections** waiting, and what is **not sent** yet. It also says when the project was last synced (in orange when nothing has been sent for over a day). **Open the map** and **Sync now** are large buttons, so they are easy to press outdoors; Sync says how many items it will send.
